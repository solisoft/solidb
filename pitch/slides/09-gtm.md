## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Developer Community (Now)

- **GitHub presence** — Open source, MIT license
- **8 client libraries** — Meet developers where they are
- **Hackathons & Meetups** — Sponsor and speak
- **Content marketing** — Tutorials, benchmarks, use cases

### Phase 2: Early Adopters (Q2 2026)

- **Reference customers** — 10 paying accounts
- **Cloud beta** — Managed offering for validation
- **Integration partners** — Lua frameworks, AI tools

### Phase 3: Scale (Q4 2026)

- **Enterprise sales** — Dedicated AE for large accounts
- **Marketplace** — AWS, GCP, Azure listings
- **AI partnerships** — LLM providers, embedding services

- Single binary, zero dependencies
- Familiar SDBQL syntax (ArangoDB-inspired)
- Built-in dashboard and REPL
