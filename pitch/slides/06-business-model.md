## Business Model: Open-Core

### License Strategy

| Tier | License | Price |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Self-Hosted** | O'Saasy License | Free |
| **Cloud** | Subscription | $99/mo per node |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Contact us |

### Revenue Streams

1. **Managed Cloud Service** — Fully hosted SoliDB, zero ops
2. **Enterprise Support** — SLA guarantees, dedicated support
3. **Custom Integrations** — AI bindings, specialized features

### Why the O'Saasy License?

- Permissive for development and self-hosting
- Non-compete clause protects against cloud competitors
- Similar to BSL/SQLite dual-license model

### Target Customers

- Startups building modern web/mobile apps
- Scale-ups needing cost-effective scaling
- Enterprises requiring self-hosted AI-ready databases
